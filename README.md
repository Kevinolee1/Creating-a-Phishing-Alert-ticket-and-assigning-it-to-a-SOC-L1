# Creating-a-Phishing-Alert-ticket-and-assigning-it-to-a-SOC-L1
A phishing alert was generated after a suspicious email was reported by a user.
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Creating-a-Phishing-Alert-ticket-and-assigning-it-to-a-SOC-L1/blob/cbb69dac0d3d36b0f11037173df4e73c9fde9560/Screenshot%202026-08-18%20191322.png)
Click “Open New Ticket” to initiate the ticket creation process.
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Creating-a-Phishing-Alert-ticket-and-assigning-it-to-a-SOC-L1/blob/bdea33d8bd262413f350644e24245b14274f5671/Screenshot%202026-08-16%20042034.png)
Enter the victim’s name and email address, then select Security Incident as the Help Topic. Provide a clear and concise description of the incident in the Issue Summary field, including the primary concern and relevant details
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Creating-a-Phishing-Alert-ticket-and-assigning-it-to-a-SOC-L1/blob/85e45253ede163e64d20d06052a6855e781f3e20/Screenshot%202026-08-16%20041914.png)
Enter the phishing alert details, including the issue summary, relevant email information, and identified Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Creating-a-Phishing-Alert-ticket-and-assigning-it-to-a-SOC-L1/blob/c51d75f9c0c823562f1730f30c5323f4417aa8c5/Screenshot%202026-08-18%20011200.png)
Sign in with your administrator credentials.
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Creating-a-Phishing-Alert-ticket-and-assigning-it-to-a-SOC-L1/blob/3184e47ec5895cb3fe7df2105e3255a8c728027d/Screenshot%202026-08-16%20042152.png) 
Change the ticket priority to High to reflect the potential severity and impact of the incident.
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Creating-a-Phishing-Alert-ticket-and-assigning-it-to-a-SOC-L1/blob/4ef5d47ff93ee4b2729ea50a148db202c5c0df58/Screenshot%202026-08-16%20042218.png)
Assign the ticket to a SOC Level 1 Analyst for initial triage, alert validation, and investigation.
